      LMNTRIX

      Private space. Inner circle.

      LMNTRIX is a front-end concept for a small, private room shared by a close group of friends. It is not a social network, not a workplace tool, and not a messaging platform.

      This build is a visual prototype. There is no server, no networking layer, and no account system. Labels such as “ENCRYPTED UI” and “CONNECTED” are interface styling only — they do not describe real security or connectivity features.

      Everything you type is stored locally in your browser so the interface feels alive between visits. Clearing your browser data removes it.
